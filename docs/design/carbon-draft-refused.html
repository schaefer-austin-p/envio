Labels
Not uploaded: never tried yet, or offline (as in #228 today).
Mailbox full: [OVERQUOTA].
Drafts read-only: [NOPERM], or a NO that reads as no permission. A best guess.
Too large: [TOOBIG] or size wording in the NO. [LIMIT] stays busy and shows "Not uploaded".
Server refused: any other NO.
Local refusals (a failed validation or APPEND) show "Server refused" too.
Retries back off: 5 minutes, doubling, up to 1 hour. A new save or a manual refresh starts over.
The pill is a new 24 px status component, not Chip.qml. Its dark-mode ink comes from theme tokens.
Lost compared with B: the longer advice, such as “Free up space to save it.” It could move into C’s banner later.
