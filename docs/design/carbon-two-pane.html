Narrow, with the compose from frame 2
The compose is now the top page of the stack. Close (X) pops back to the list.
  • Crossing the threshold in either direction is live: no restart, no reload.
  • Survives the switch: the selected message; the reader scroll position; an open compose, which becomes the top page when narrowing and the right pane when widening, with its draft as saved.
  • Narrowing with the reader open (frame 1): the reader becomes the top page over the list. Widening from a list-only stack: the right pane shows the selected message, or the empty state if none.
  • QML only arranges the panes. The selection, paging and scroll state stay in envio-ui.

Keyboard shortcuts
A small dialog opened with ? or from Settings. The shortcuts are the ones in the issue.
  • NEW Hover fill: light #F0EDE5 (dark #1B2027), proposed as a theme role "hover". Everything else uses current roles.
  • NEW Context menu: an anchored popup on the dialog surface (white, field-border outline, 8 px corners) holding the long-press actions.
  • NEW Keyboard-shortcut dialog: the existing dialog (title, rows, outlined Close) with key caps drawn as small outlined chips in IBM Plex Mono.
  • Keyboard focus follows the selected row. A Tab focus ring is the platform's and is not drawn here.
New components and open questions
New, for the implementer
  • Close (X) in the reader top bar for the wide layout (Back stays in the stack).
  • Empty reader state.
  • Hover fill role.
  • Context menu popup.
  • Keyboard-shortcut dialog.
Open, for the owner
  • 1. Drawer: keep it a drawer on two panes (frames 1 to 3) and add the third column only from about 180 gu (1440 px)? Frame 4 assumes yes.
  • 2. Clicking another row while compose is open: keep compose, move only the selection? Or ask Discard or Save draft first? Frame 2 assumes keep.
  • 3. Left pane width: fixed 400 px (50 gu) as drawn, or a draggable splitter?
  • 4. Reader width: text capped at 640 px with margin; images fit to that measure (#172, #170), font clamp (#184, #191) unchanged.
  • 5. Shortcuts dialog: opened by ? and from Settings; is that enough?
